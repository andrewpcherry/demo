Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does answering the questions commit me to anything?+
No. This helps us understand your situation so the first conversation is actually useful. There is no obligation, no listing of your information for sale, and no pressure to move forward.
Are you an insurance company or a public adjuster?+
No. Gulf Coast Contractors is a licensed general contractor. We work with insurance companies and adjusters every day and document your damage thoroughly, but decisions about your claim rest with you and your carrier. Reporting the damage to your insurer is your first step, we can't do it for you, and nothing here is insurance or legal advice.
What does a documented inspection include?+
A complete look at the roof and any interior damage, with photos of what we find. If a claim is involved, that documentation and our estimate are prepared in the format your adjuster works with.
What is IINK?+
IINK is a claims-payment platform we partner with. Insurance checks often have to pass through your mortgage company, and each stage can take 5 to 7 days. With IINK, a process that can take up to six weeks can be cut to just a few days, with real-time updates along the way.
Do you offer financing?+
Yes. Financing options are available, and pre-qualification is fast, easy and doesn't affect your credit score.
What areas do you serve?+
Our primary service area is all of Southern Louisiana: the Northshore, the Southshore, Greater New Orleans, Baton Rouge and the Lake Charles area. We also serve Florida from our Bradenton location.
How fast will someone contact me?+
You'll get an immediate confirmation when your request comes in, and a fast follow-up to get your inspection or review scheduled. If you have an active leak, flooding or storm emergency, call (985) 250-9322 directly. Emergency response runs 24/7.
