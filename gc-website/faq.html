What areas do you serve?
Our primary service area includes all of Southern Louisiana: the Northshore, the Southshore, the Baton Rouge area, the Lake Charles area and more. We also serve the state of Florida from our Bradenton location.
What sets you apart from other contractors in Southern Louisiana?
We are insurance work experts. We use the same estimating software as insurance companies and adjusters, so we can file supplements in the format your adjuster needs and move your claim along faster than other contractors.
What is the IICRC?
The Institute of Inspection Cleaning and Restoration Certification (IICRC) is the leading authority in the inspection, cleaning and restoration industries. We hold two IICRC certifications, Water Damage Restoration Technician (WRT) and Applied Structural Drying (ASD).
What is IINK?
IINK is a claims-payment platform we partner with. Insurance checks often have to pass through your mortgage company, and each stage can take 5 to 7 days. With IINK, a process that can take up to six weeks can be cut to just a few days, with real-time updates and FedEx tracking.
Can you report my damage to my insurance company?
No. Reporting the damage is your first step: contact your insurance provider and explain what happened. If you are directed to file a claim, a field adjuster will usually schedule an inspection, and we can work with the adjuster from there.
Do you offer emergency service?
Yes. We offer 24/7 emergency response: roof tarping, board-up, structural stabilization and water mitigation to prevent further damage.
What roofing warranties do you offer?
As a GAF Certified contractor, every GAF architectural shingle roof we install qualifies for the lifetime GAF Shingle & Accessory Limited Warranty. We also offer the enhanced System Plus Limited Warranty, with no maximum wind speed limit and coverage extended to 50 years.
Do you offer financing?
Yes. Financing options are available, and pre-qualification is fast, easy and doesn’t affect your credit score.
