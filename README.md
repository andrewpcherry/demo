# Insurance Growth & Performance Demo

An interactive product concept for insurance agencies, connecting marketing, producer development, follow-through and financial results.

## Explore

- Marketing channels and optional automation workflows
- Producer preparation, call review, lessons and practice
- Agency and producer financial reporting
- Role previews, customer journeys and executive actions
- Original and V2 partner decks for comparison

All records, transcripts, financial figures and AI responses are fictional. Workflow actions are simulated; no messages are sent and no customer account is connected. Changes last only for the current page session. Role switching illustrates the proposed experience, not production access control.

The V2 deck includes on-demand intelligence, custom KPIs, agency memory and a mobile managed agent. Those broader capabilities are a product concept, not a claim of live implementation.
